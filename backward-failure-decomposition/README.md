# The Ghosts in the Machine

**Backward Failure Decomposition (BFD) is a method for tracing a documented AI failure backward through its model-development lineage, progressively decomposing it until the smallest identifiable problem capable of contributing to the final incident is found.**

**Tracing AI Failures Backward** — Backward Failure Decomposition (BFD), integrated research monograph, v1.1 (10 October 2026), R. Rajan.

**[Read the complete monograph](MONOGRAPH.md)**

The work comprises one foundational theory, four case-specific backward investigations, comparative synthesis, and a research programme. These are exploratory applications, not conclusive demonstrations of universal ancestral failures.

## Evidence boundary

**Primary track:** backward reconstruction in the target model-development family, including dated model-stage observations, safeguards, and supported research/method transmission bridges. **Secondary track:** unrelated historical analogues, kept separate and never treated as proof of ancestry. Open questions and missing links are retained.

Status: public research preprint; not peer-reviewed. The existing repository's earlier papers are independent from this monograph.
