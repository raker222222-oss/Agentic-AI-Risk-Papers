Rogue Without Will

Human Intent, Technological Leverage, and Frontier AI · Rakesh Rajan

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

This paper separates three different risk categories that are often blurred together: unintended agentic failure, deliberate human-directed misuse, and claims of independent machine volition.

Its central argument is that dangerous outcomes do not require a machine to possess human-like desire or will. A human can already supply motive and intent, while frontier AI contributes capability, speed, scale, automation, and access.

Historical examples from phone phreaking, hacking, worms, botnets, and insider misuse illustrate a recurring pattern: new communications technologies can amplify the operational reach of individuals faster than institutions learn to contain them.

Core proposition

Danger does not require will. Goal-directed behavior is not the same as independent machine will, and human intent combined with frontier-model capability can itself create disproportionate risk.
