Evidentiary Threshold Distortion in Agentic AI

Rakesh Rajan · Working paper

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

AI systems can fail not only by acting on too little evidence, but also by demanding a level of proof that is inappropriate for practical decision-making. When multiple weak signals converge, an agent may still underreact if it treats courtroom-level proof as a prerequisite for operational action.

This paper proposes Evidentiary Threshold Distortion: a mismatch between the evidence threshold used by an AI system and the threshold appropriate to the decision at hand.

Why it matters

In safety-critical environments, waiting for explicit proof can itself be dangerous. Practical action thresholds often differ from proof thresholds, especially when delay carries asymmetric cost.
