Open-World Retrieval Failure in Agentic AI

Rakesh Rajan · Working paper

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

Real-world AI agents operate in open environments where novel states can create knowledge gaps the agent was never explicitly prepared to recognize. Retrieval can then fail through omission, substitution, staleness, conflict, contamination, or failure to recognize that retrieval is needed at all.

This paper develops a model of open-world retrieval failure in which a novel state produces a knowledge deficit, imperfect retrieval, false epistemic closure, coherent reasoning, and then wrong real-world action.

It also examines source-selection recursion, unknown unknowns, Black Swan conditions, and the need to retrieve not just information but current state: content, order, revision history, and present validity.

Core claim

Not retrieved does not mean does not exist. An autonomous agent must evaluate whether the information it has found is actually sufficient before acting.
