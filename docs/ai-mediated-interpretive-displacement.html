AI-Mediated Interpretive Displacement

Rakesh Rajan · Working paper

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

Generative AI is increasingly used not only to draft messages but to interpret them. A person can respond to an AI-generated interpretation rather than to the original communication, allowing the model to enter the causal chain of a relationship or decision process.

This paper proposes AI-Mediated Interpretive Displacement (AMID): a shift in which the operative meaning of a human communication moves from the original exchange to an AI-generated interpretation. The safety concern is not merely hallucination. An interpretation can change later behavior and become causally consequential even when the original interpretation was wrong.

The paper develops a sequence model of this process, distinguishes interpretation from evidence, identifies reciprocal AI-mediated escalation, examines context compression and anomaly failure, and proposes falsifiable experiments across models and prompting conditions.

Why it matters for agentic AI

In an autonomous system, an ambiguous input can be interpreted incorrectly, acted upon, and converted into a new world state. Later reasoning may then treat that state as confirmation of the original interpretation. Wrong interpretation can therefore acquire operational authority without malicious intent.
