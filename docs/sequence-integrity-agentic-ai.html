Sequence Integrity as an Agentic AI Safety Problem

Rakesh Rajan · Working paper

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

Agentic systems must preserve not only facts but also chronology, precedence, procedural order, and revision history. Correct information arranged in the wrong sequence can describe the wrong state of the world and lead an autonomous system to take the wrong action.

This paper treats sequence integrity as a distinct AI safety requirement. It examines how agents can mis-handle superseded instructions, reordered events, stale state, and long-context timelines even when individual facts are accurate.

Why it matters

Tool-using agents act on state. If sequence is corrupted, the state model can be wrong without any individual datum being false. This creates risks in workflows, memory, instruction updates, compliance, and long-horizon planning.
