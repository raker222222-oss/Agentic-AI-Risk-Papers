Recursive Amplification of Interpretive Failure in Agentic AI

Rakesh Rajan · Working paper

All papersFull paper on GitHubDOI

Abstract

Agentic AI systems observe, infer, act, receive new information, and reason again. This creates a specific safety problem: an initially wrong interpretation can alter the environment, and the altered environment can then be treated as new evidence supporting the original mistake.

This paper proposes Recursive Amplification of Interpretive Failure (RAIF): initial interpretive error → action based on error → state change → self-generated corroboration → increased confidence → further action.

The danger is not independent machine malice. It is recursive reasoning increasing the authority of a mistaken starting assumption.

Key safety problem

Single-turn errors may end with one answer. Agentic errors can propagate through tool use and state changes, making later observations partly products of the system's own mistaken actions.
